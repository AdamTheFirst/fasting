# Brand: fasting app landscape and our identity

Made 2026-10-30 with the replica-brand method, on angle B from [fixes.md](fixes.md): **a tracker for people who already know how they fast**, with "no cap on length" as the headline proof.

"Fast me" is the working title. The section on names below explains why it should not be the store name.

### The pattern

1. **Everyone sells weight loss.** Four of the five biggest put "Weight Loss" or "lose fat" in the name, subtitle or hero. Fasting is the method, the body is the product.
2. **Everyone is drifting away from fasting.** Zero is now "Fasting & Protein", Fastic is "Protein, Tracker, Nutrition", Simple is an AI coach. The pure fasting tool is being abandoned by the leaders.
3. **Everyone is a coach.** The app knows best: plans, contracts, AI coaches, "journeys". That is exactly what an experienced faster does not want.
4. **Everyone shouts.** "#1", "50 million", "millions of pounds lost", "works!". Claims nobody can check.
5. **Names all lean on "fast".** Fastic, BodyFast, DoFasting, FitFast, FastEasy, MyFast, Fasty, Fasta, FastTrack, SuperFast, JustFast, Dayfast, "Fast:", "fast_" all exist in the stores (seen in search results today). It is the most crowded word in the category.
6. **Everyone draws a ring.** The circular countdown is the hero of almost every fasting app's main screen and many icons. It also quietly says "16 hours, then done", which is the opposite of our promise. (This is from general knowledge of the apps, not a screenshot check run today; the design thread can confirm.)

## 2. White space we can own

- **A fasting tool, not a weight-loss product.** Say "fasting" and mean it. No body talk, no before/after. This also matches the spec's non-goal of not marketing for weight loss, and keeps Apple 1.4.1 risk low.
- **Quiet confidence instead of hype.** No "#1", no counts of users. The proof is a screenshot of `9d 14h 02m` running.
- **Your rules.** The one brand in the category that trusts the user. Not a coach, a logbook.
- **Days, not rings.** A visual language built on lines and days (a long horizon, a calendar of filled days) instead of a 24-hour circle.
- **Honest money as a line in the listing**, not the identity (per fixes.md, angle C).

Positioning line: **"For people who already know how to fast."**

Tagline options (fresh, none echo a competitor):
- "Your fast. Your rules. No ceiling."
- "Fast as long as you mean to."
- "The fasting log that stays out of your way."

## 3. The name

### Why not "Fast me"

- It sits in the most crowded word in the category, next to **MyFast** ("my fast" and "fast me" are the same two words), "Fast:", FastEasy and a dozen others. That is the "confusingly similar" test the skill applies, and it fails it against MyFast.
- "Fast" alone is close to descriptive for a fasting app, so it is weak as a trademark.
- In speech, "fast me" sounds like a request ("fast me through this").

It is a fine internal codename. It should not be the store name.

### 20 candidates

| Style | Candidates |
| --- | --- |
| Descriptive | Longhaul, Daylong, Logfast, Openended |
| Compound | Daycount, Stillday, Hourline, Keepwell |
| Invented | Fermo, Veyla, Ayun, Nofa |
| Metaphor | Fallow, Ebb, Vigil, Keel, Horizon, Ballast |
| Verb | Hold, Stint |

Cut immediately: anything containing "fast" (crowded), **Stint** (an existing "Stint: OMAD Fasting Tracker"), **Daycount** (taken by day-counter apps, plus "Dayfast" exists), **Keel** (a "Keel weight tracker" exists in health), **Horizon** (too common everywhere), **Ayun** and **Nofa** (meanings in other languages not checked, too risky for a first pass).

### The five

| Name | Why it fits | Watch out |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Fallow** (top pick) | A field left fallow rests on purpose and comes back stronger. Rest by choice, owned by the farmer, measured in seasons not hours. Calm, uncommon, nothing like any competitor | Can also mean "idle". Spell it once out loud and people get it |
| **Longhaul** | Says the headline feature in one word: built for the long ones | Leans on angle A more than B; "long haul" is also used for COVID and trucking |
| **Ebb** | Three letters, strong icon potential, the tide going out and coming back | Can read as "decline" ("energy ebbing"); short words are hard to get as domains |
| **Vigil** | Staying with something on purpose, through the night. Serious, adult | Strong religious tone; several prayer apps already use "Vigil" |
| **Hold** | Verb, plain, "hold the fast". Fits the quiet voice | Very generic, hard to trademark and to search for |

## 4. Palette

Everyone in the category reaches for "healthy" hues: greens, teals, bright blue, warm orange and coral. None of that was verified to hex today (the brand sites and stores didn't expose colours to my tools), so check against the real icons. We go elsewhere: **plum on oat**, earthy and quiet, which also fits "Fallow".

## 5. Logo brief

- **Idea:** a horizon that keeps going. Three horizontal strokes like furrows in a resting field, the bottom one longer than the frame, so the mark itself has no end. Ties to "Fallow" and to "no ceiling".
- **Type:** symbol plus wordmark. The symbol alone is the app icon.
- **Wordmark:** lowercase "fallow", a humanist sans or soft serif, no ligature tricks.
- **Must work** at 16px (favicon: the strokes become three bars) and as a 1024px app icon (plum strokes on oat, or oat on plum).
- **Deliverables:** SVG master, iOS icon 1024x1024 with no transparency, Android adaptive icon, favicon set (16, 32, 180, 512), social image 1200x630.
- **Must not resemble:** any ring, circle, clock face, stopwatch or "0". No fork and knife, no leaf, no flame, no body silhouette. Put the drafts next to the Zero, Fastic, BodyFast, Simple and LIFE icons before choosing.

## 6. Voice

**Plain, steady, trusting.**

- **Plain**, not dull: short words, numbers first, no hype.
- **Steady**, not stern: calm about long fasts, never alarmed, never cheering.
- **Trusting**, not careless: you decide, and we still tell you the safety facts once, clearly.

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| "3d 4h" | "You're crushing it! 🔥" |
| "Ended. 72h 10m." | "Congratulations on completing your fasting journey!" |
| Say what a milestone usually means and link the source | "Fat burning mode activated" as fact |
| Ask nothing you don't need | "Tell us about your goals" |
| Let a broken fast be data | "You broke your streak" |

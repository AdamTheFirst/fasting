# Wording rules

Fastaway is a wellness log, not a medical device. Every word in the app, notifications, store listing,
website and social posts follows these rules. They keep the app on the right side of Apple 1.4.1,
Google Play's health policy, FDA general-wellness guidance and the EU MDR.

1. **Say "likely", cite a source.** Every statement about the body says "likely" and links its source,
   as the milestone cards do. No "you are now burning fat".
2. **Show numbers, never verdicts.** No zones, targets, colours that judge a value, or "too low" / "you need
   more". Body signals are drawn as they are, with no "recovery" or "stress" labels.
3. **No disease words.** Never heal, treat, cure, detox, reverse, prevent, therapeutic, or any condition
   (diabetes, cancer, epilepsy...). No weight-loss promises.
4. **No pressure.** No "beat your record", badges for length, "don't break the chain" or reminders to keep
   going. A gap is just data.
5. **The user's words are the user's.** Lists and notes the user writes are never pre-filled with
   app-written advice (food, doses, amounts).
6. **Safety stays free and visible.** The safety card, the doctor list and the helpline link are never
   behind the paywall and never hidden by default.
7. **No data claims we can't keep.** "Nothing leaves your phone" stays true only while no dependency talks to
   a server. Before adding one, update the privacy policy (`src/config/privacy.ts` and `docs/PRIVACY.md`)
   and the store privacy labels.
